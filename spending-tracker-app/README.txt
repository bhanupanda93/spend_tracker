Spend Tracker (offline iPhone app) - version 10
1. Upload this whole folder to a free HTTPS host (Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages).
2. Open the link in Safari on your iPhone while online.
3. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen.
4. Open the app once from the home screen while online. After that it works offline.
Open it from the hosted link, not by previewing index.html in the Files app.
EMI and card reminders: Calendar & EMI reminders > "Add reminders to iPhone Calendar" saves a .ics file. Open it and tap Add All so iOS alerts you offline.
